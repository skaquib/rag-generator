# Nimbus X2 Air Purifier — User Manual

## Specifications

- Coverage: rooms up to 45 square metres
- Clean air delivery rate (CADR): 310 m³/h
- Noise level: 24 dB in Sleep mode, 52 dB in Turbo mode
- Power consumption: 38 W maximum
- Filter: 3-stage (pre-filter, H13 HEPA, activated carbon)

## Modes

The Nimbus X2 has four modes: Sleep, Auto, Normal and Turbo. In Auto mode the built-in PM2.5 sensor adjusts the fan speed every 30 seconds. The indicator ring glows blue when air quality is good, yellow when moderate and red when poor.

## Filter replacement

Replace the combined HEPA/carbon filter every 6 months, or sooner when the filter light turns orange. The pre-filter can be vacuumed every 2 weeks and should not be washed with water. Use only genuine filter model NX-F2.

To reset the filter indicator, hold the Timer button for 5 seconds until the device beeps twice.

## Troubleshooting

- Error E1: the front cover is not closed properly. Re-seat the cover.
- Error E2: the fan motor is blocked. Unplug the unit and contact support.
- Strange smell: the carbon layer is saturated; replace the filter.

## Warranty

The Nimbus X2 has a 2-year limited warranty from the date of purchase. Filters are consumables and are not covered by the warranty.
